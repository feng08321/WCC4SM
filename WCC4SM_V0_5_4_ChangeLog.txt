WCC4SM V0.5.4
Date: 2026-08-03

Purpose
-------
Third low-risk modularization release. WCC4SM_V0_5_3.m remains unchanged as
the operational fallback baseline.

Changes
-------
1. Calibrated peak-center wavelength, wavelength-domain FWHM and ERW now come
   from wc4sm_calculate_calibrated_performance.
2. FWHM-ERW relation statistics and FWHM summary statistics come from the same
   pure calculation module.
3. Pixel wavelength interval and its summary statistics come from the module.
4. Existing plots, histogram range/bin controls and OPEN FIG behavior remain in
   the GUI and retain their V0.5.3 presentation.
5. Earlier spectrum, preprocessing, calibration and LOO modules remain active.

Validation status
-----------------
The calibrated-performance module passed the 26-test non-GUI regression suite
before integration. V0.5.4 requires the same suite and comparison of all four
Calibrated Performance plots against V0.5.3.
